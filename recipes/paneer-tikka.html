Paneer Tikka

A delicious Indian dish made with grilled cottage cheese cubes marinated in spices.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Cube the paneer and set aside.
  2. Mix yogurt, garlic, ginger, lemon juice, and all the spices to create the marinade.
  3. Marinate the paneer cubes in the mixture for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat the grill or skillet and cook the paneer until golden brown.
