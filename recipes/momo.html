Momo

A delicious Nepali dish made with steamed dumplings filled with meat or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 lb ground beef or vegetables
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili oil

Steps

  1. Mix flour with water to make a thick dough and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Prepare the filling by mixing ground beef or vegetables with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and chili oil.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut into circles. Place a small amount of filling in the center and fold to create a pleated shape.
  4. Steam the momos for 10-15 minutes until cooked through.
