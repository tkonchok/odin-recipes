Lasagna

A delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package directions.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
